# Modern Fundamental Analyst 1.0.0

Modern Fundamental Analyst is a public-equity research website built on a belief that **investing is for everyone**. Its mission is to empower every retail investor. Readers can examine a stock thesis, see which businesses the portfolio owns, and follow its performance against the SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) month by month. The site is available in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. [Visit Modern Fundamental Analyst](https://www.modernfundamentalanalyst.com)

## The investment approach

I use **value investing** for stock pitching and **focus investing** for portfolio management. I aim to stay within my circle of competence and understand what I own. I treat accounting as the language of business and use three-statement and discounted cash flow models to estimate intrinsic value. That same discipline guides how I study AI and other disruptive technologies.

Patience and discipline give compounding time to work. Monthly disclosure makes that belief accountable: readers can compare my investment reasoning with the positions I hold and see the results, including months when the portfolio loses value. I approach the research with a “learn-it-all” mindset grounded in curiosity, empathy and continuous learning. [About the analyst](https://www.modernfundamentalanalyst.com/about)

### Understand the performance record

The Performance page pairs each chart with a monthly table. Two measures answer different questions. [View performance and methodology](https://www.modernfundamentalanalyst.com/performance)

| Reader question                                                               | Measure                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How did the investments perform over time?                                    | Reconstructed time-weighted return (TWR) links returns across periods while accounting for flows into the invested portfolio. The chart compares cumulative portfolio TWR with SPY total return.     |
| What annualized return corresponds to the timing and size of the investments? | Since-inception XIRR weights dated cash flows. The benchmark simulates investing in SPY on the corresponding purchase dates. Each monthly observation reports the annualized result up to that date. |

The figures cover listed stocks and ETFs and account for trading fees, net dividends and financing interest. Cash balances, separate brokerage fund positions and liabilities are outside the valuation boundary. The TWR reconstruction uses closing prices. By October month-end the portfolio had gained 31.30% cumulatively against 31.00% for SPY, including a −5.04% portfolio return in October. ## Reading and navigating the site

A Swiss-style 12-column editorial grid aligns headings, commentary and financial data on larger screens. On smaller screens, editorial sections stack, memo cards reflow and wide financial tables remain scrollable. Major sections use 64px padding and 48px between content groups. Homepage return bars share a zero line so readers can compare small gains and see losses extend downward.

Jost and Noto Sans support Latin and Chinese text with a shared Perfect Fourth type scale. Simplified Chinese fonts load in smaller files while retaining the original glyph coverage. Light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, keyboard controls and localized status messages carry across the site. [Design System](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)

## How the project works

- **Research and publishing.** Next.js App Router, React and TypeScript run on Node.js 24. Portfolio records and calculations live in a versioned feature module. Investment memos are authored in MDX and rendered on the server, with article typography loaded on memo routes. Localized metadata, canonical URLs, language alternates and a sitemap help readers discover and share public pages. [Web Application Architecture](WEB_APPLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Reader services.** Resend handles contact and subscription email. Upstash Redis coordinates requests, consent, duplicate-event handling and delivery suppression. Stripe handles one-time payments. Sentry collects errors and performance signals with privacy filters, text and input masking, and Replay exclusions for sensitive pages. [Integrations](INTEGRATIONS.md)
- **Quality and delivery.** Unit and integration tests cover calculations, components and service behavior. Playwright checks reader journeys in Chromium and WebKit. GitHub Actions enforces calibrated performance budgets and records native Lighthouse Baseline diagnostics. The Vercel production gate checks CI for the exact release commit. [Release workflow](WEB_APPLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#release-workflow)

## Known Limitations

- Scheduled uptime monitoring remains unverified after Sentry rejected activation; SDK telemetry is separate from uptime coverage.
- Portfolio returns use closing-price reconstructions rather than observed intraday valuations.
- Failed welcome deliveries require reconciliation; an automatic replay worker is not included.
- TypeScript 7 and ESLint 10 upgrades remain constrained by required lint-plugin compatibility. A development-only dependency advisory remains tracked in the [upgrade constraints](WEB_APPLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#upgrade-constraints).
- Physical iPhone acceptance remains unverified; automated WebKit tests do not establish physical-device compatibility.

Release acceptance follows the [architecture release workflow](WEB_APPLICATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#release-workflow). Native Baseline is diagnostic; local Lighthouse budgets, browser tests and SEO metadata checks do not establish field performance, historical-browser compatibility or search ranking gains.
