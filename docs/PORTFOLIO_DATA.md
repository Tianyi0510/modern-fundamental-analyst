# Portfolio Data

This guide owns the portfolio data model, calculation scope, reconciliation, and update procedure.

## Workspace data

`src/features/portfolio/portfolio-detail.ts` contains 91 purchase lots with fees, a shared table of SPY adjusted prices by applied trading date, 124 dated dividend, withholding-tax, and financing-interest cash events, three corporate actions, and 22 month-end position, price, and XIRR snapshots. Amounts and prices are in USD; dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`. Cash-event amounts are signed: dividends are positive, taxes and interest are negative. Purchase costs are `grossAmount + fees`. Simulated SPY units use `grossAmount / adjustedClose`, while the full purchase cost is the simulated cash outflow; they are hypothetical holdings.

`src/features/portfolio/portfolio.ts` derives the website's current holdings, cost basis, income, market value, and return history from those records. It also exports monthly simulated SPY units, values, and since-inception XIRRs through `portfolioSpyMonthlyReturns`, plus the latest comparison through `portfolioSpySnapshot`. Home, Portfolio, and Performance import this shared module. The application reads workspace data directly and does not need a database or a separate data-processing service.

The latest month-end valuation sets `asOf` for the published snapshot. Later purchases and cash events do not enter its cost basis, income or return; simulated SPY units include a purchase only after both its transaction date and applied price date. Detailed future records may be prepared before the next valuation without changing the currently published snapshot.

The published 2026-09-30 snapshot contains 18 holdings and 22 month-end observations. Stocks total USD 127,533.11; the simulated SPY value is USD 121,237.01. XIRR and TWR share the same stock-investment boundary: cash balances, fund holdings and liability balances are excluded. Net dividends, withholding tax, trading fees and financing interest remain included; excluding liability balances does not remove interest expense. Account deposits, withdrawals and currency exchanges are outside this boundary. Here, excluded funds mean the separate brokerage fund-account positions; listed ETFs already included in the published stock ledger, such as VGT and GRNY, remain included to preserve the existing XIRR scope. The Performance chart shows since-inception annualized XIRR at each month-end, not each month's standalone return; horizons below 30 days remain unavailable.

## Calculation and updates

Keep source precision until display formatting. Portfolio cumulative return includes net dividends and deducts financing interest; individual holding returns compare market value with cost basis and do not allocate portfolio-level interest. The dated events support other cash-flow calculations, while the month-end prices and quantities support position-level valuation. Simulated SPY units and terminal values are derived from the recorded inputs; the XIRR observations are stored results, not recomputed by the website at request time.

Each month records the applied U.S. trading date, which precedes the calendar month-end when markets are closed. SPY is the only benchmark. Preserve previously published monthly prices and XIRRs unless the source evidence supports an explicit correction.

## Reconstructed time-weighted return

`portfolio-closing-marks.ts` stores 450 daily/event-date marks and 6,752 held-position closing prices from 2024-12-30 through 2026-09-30. Public Yahoo Finance chart data was retrieved on 2026-10-05. Stock inputs use split-adjusted **close**, not dividend-adjusted close, because the signed cash-event ledger records dividends and tax separately. VGT prices before its 2026-04-21 split are multiplied by eight to match the contemporaneous share count. Non-trading input dates use the preceding U.S. trading close and retain that applied date. All 22 month-end stock valuations reconcile within USD 0.011. Original broker files and account identifiers remain outside the repository.

`portfolio-twr-calculations.ts` reconstructs the **stock sleeve**, not the full brokerage account. Every purchase contributes its gross amount plus fees at the beginning of its valuation period. Gifted securities contribute fair value using the preceding closing mark; they are not investment gains. Splits only change quantities. Net dividends, withholding taxes and financing costs settle at period end and are included in performance before being removed from the sleeve. As with XIRR, cash balances, fund holdings and liability balances are excluded. Account deposits and withdrawals are outside this measurement boundary; stock purchases and in-kind transfers are contributions to the stock sleeve.

For each valuation period, `factor = (ending stock value + signed net income) / (preceding stock value + contributions)`. Geometrically link factors for cumulative TWR. Each monthly return is the ratio of successive cumulative growth factors; the first monthly period includes the initial investment and fees. Missing event or held-position prices, unvalued transfers and invalid ordering stop calculation rather than producing a plausible value. These are reconstructed closing-price periods under explicit beginning/end assumptions. They do not recover the actual intraday value when a flow occurred. Historical trade dates recorded on weekends remain on their recorded date with the preceding close.

`portfolio-twr.ts` runs only on the server and exports reconstructed period valuations, monthly returns and the latest summary. The historical price table must not enter a client bundle. The latest cumulative stock-sleeve TWR is 38.2694002516289%, and September's linked monthly return is 5.2341595775561744%. SPY's comparison is 32.19753204498825%, using one common vintage of dividend-adjusted closes from the first purchase date, with no synthetic purchase-fee deduction. It is an index total-return comparison, not the existing same-investment-date XIRR simulation. Existing XIRR observations and their frozen benchmark-price basis are unchanged.

Performance displays a separate reconstructed TWR summary, chart and monthly cumulative data table. Both TWR series are **cumulative and not annualized**. Three-language methodology copy names the valuation and flow assumptions. [GIPS calculation methodology](https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/) explains geometrically linked sub-period returns and beginning/end flow conventions; the website does not claim GIPS compliance or observed intraday TWR. The stock-only boundary is selected to match XIRR, not to represent a GIPS composite carve-out: Provision 3.A.15 requires cash and related income for carve-outs included in composites. Exact flow-time TWR would still require actual valuations and event ordering at every external flow.

When updating, refresh closing marks through the new month, record source and retrieval date, verify split basis and every monthly position value, and run the adjacent `portfolio-twr*.test.mjs` tests. Refresh the entire SPY TWR price vintage consistently; do not mix adjustment vintages. Preserve the independently maintained published XIRR history. Tests cover geometric linking, added contributions, fees, dividends/tax/interest, in-kind transfers, splits, non-trading dates, missing inputs and month-end reconciliation.

## Update and acceptance procedure

1. Identify the input revision before editing. In the change or release record, note the snapshot month, the source provider or dataset name and its available version or retrieval date, the price-adjustment basis, and the evidence review date. Use a non-private batch label if the transaction source has no public version. The Git commit identifies the published workspace-data revision. Keep statements, account identifiers, raw source files and local paths out of the repository and release record.
2. Add or correct dated purchases, cash events, corporate actions, applied SPY adjusted closes and month-end valuations in `src/features/portfolio/portfolio-detail.ts`. Use the actual transaction date for purchases and the applied U.S. trading date for SPY prices; record both when they differ. Keep source precision until display formatting. Change `src/features/portfolio/portfolio.ts` only if derivation or presentation rules change.
3. Reconcile against the reviewed inputs: purchase `shares × unitPrice = grossAmount`; purchase cost includes fees; signed cash events sum to net dividends and financing interest; corporate actions and purchases reproduce every month-end share count; `shares × price` reproduces stock value; each SPY purchase price matches its applied date; simulated units and terminal value reproduce the benchmark; stored portfolio and SPY XIRRs match the reviewed cash flows. The test independently recomputes SPY XIRR and checks that the latest stored portfolio XIRR zeros the dated cash-flow net present value; retain independent portfolio calculation evidence in the private review record. Investigate every mismatch before changing expected test values.
4. Run `node --import ./scripts/register-server.mjs --test src/features/portfolio/portfolio.test.mjs`. Its current tolerances are USD 0.0001 for purchase arithmetic, USD 0.001 for published cost and income totals, USD 0.011 for month-end valuations and SPY values, USD 0.01 for the latest SPY snapshot, 0.000001 SPY units and 0.0001 percentage point for SPY XIRR. Dates, counts and stored XIRR pairings are exact. These tolerances accommodate floating-point arithmetic; they do not permit a conflicting source price, missing event or unexplained account difference.
5. If displayed values or chart behavior change, run `npx playwright test src/features/portfolio/performance-chart.spec.ts src/app/_components/portfolio-form-status.spec.ts --project=chromium` and inspect the three language routes. CI owns full verification; before deployment, verify successful CI for the release commit and the deployment identity as described in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#deployment-completion). Do not routinely repeat the full suite locally. Accept the update only when the reconciliation, focused checks and affected pages agree with the identified source revision; record any corrected historical observation and its reason in the change review.

The application and CI use Node.js, TypeScript, and the existing test tools. Reading, updating, and calculating from the workspace data do not require Python or SQL. A one-time external data conversion may use Python when its spreadsheet or statistical libraries materially simplify that conversion; it is not a runtime dependency or a second source of portfolio data.
